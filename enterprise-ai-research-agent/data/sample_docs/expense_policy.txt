Travel and Expense Policy

1. Travel Booking
All business travel must be booked through the approved corporate travel
portal at least 5 business days in advance. Economy class is standard for
flights under 6 hours; business class requires VP-level approval.

2. Daily Meal Allowance
Employees on domestic business travel receive a daily meal allowance of
2,500 INR. International travel allowances vary by destination and are
listed in the regional expense addendum.

3. Expense Submission
All expense claims must be submitted within 15 days of the expense being
incurred, with itemized receipts attached for any single expense over 500 INR.
Claims submitted after 30 days require manager and finance approval.

4. Reimbursement Timeline
Approved expense claims are reimbursed within 10 business days of approval,
processed through the standard payroll cycle.

5. Non-Reimbursable Items
Alcohol (except at approved client-facing events), personal entertainment,
traffic fines, and upgrades beyond policy class are not reimbursable under
any circumstances.
