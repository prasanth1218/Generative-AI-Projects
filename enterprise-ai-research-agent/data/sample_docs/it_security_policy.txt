Information Security Policy

1. Password Requirements
All employee accounts must use passwords with a minimum of 12 characters,
including uppercase, lowercase, numeric, and special characters. Passwords
must be rotated every 90 days and cannot repeat the last 5 passwords used.

2. Multi-Factor Authentication
MFA is mandatory for all access to production systems, cloud infrastructure
consoles, and any system containing customer data. SMS-based MFA is
discouraged in favor of authenticator apps or hardware keys.

3. Data Classification
Company data is classified into four levels: Public, Internal, Confidential,
and Restricted. Restricted data (customer PII, financial records) requires
encryption at rest and in transit, and access is limited to named individuals
with a documented business need.

4. Incident Response
Any suspected security incident must be reported to the security team within
1 hour of discovery. The incident response team will triage within 4 hours
and provide a written summary within 48 hours of resolution.

5. Third-Party Access
Vendors and contractors requiring system access must go through a security
review and sign a data processing agreement before credentials are issued.
Access is reviewed quarterly and revoked immediately upon contract termination.
