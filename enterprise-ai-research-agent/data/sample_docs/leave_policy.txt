Employee Leave Policy

1. Annual Leave
Full-time employees are entitled to 18 paid leave days per calendar year,
accrued monthly at a rate of 1.5 days per month. Leave must be approved by
the reporting manager at least 3 working days in advance through the HR portal.

2. Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 paid sick leave days per year. A medical certificate is
required for sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days. Sick leave cannot be
carried forward to the next year.

3. Carry Forward
Unused annual leave, up to a maximum of 5 days, may be carried forward into
the next calendar year. Any leave beyond this cap is forfeited at year-end
unless otherwise approved by HR in exceptional circumstances.

4. Parental Leave
Primary caregivers are entitled to 26 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary
caregivers are entitled to 2 weeks of paid parental leave, to be taken within
6 months of the child's birth or adoption.

5. Unpaid Leave
Employees may request unpaid leave of up to 30 days per year, subject to
manager and HR approval, for personal reasons not covered under other
leave categories.
